Research

From device physics to intelligent manufacturing.

My work combines acoustic device design, RF engineering, fabrication knowledge, measurement, and machine intelligence.

Long-term vision

My long-term goal is to bring intelligent manufacturing into every stage of micro- and nanofabrication—from device design and process planning to fabrication monitoring, wafer-level testing, and production feedback.

I envision intelligent, modern, and scalable MEMS manufacturing that can accelerate the mass production of reliable devices for communications, 6G, aerospace, and other emerging fields.

Selected projects

  • 6G RF Filters for Mobile Devices — MEMS/mmWave acoustic filter design and validation for next-generation RF front-ends.
  • Spurious-mode Suppression in RF Micro-acoustics — identification and suppression methods for high-frequency resonators and filters.
  • GaN/AlScN Hetero-integration — process and RF microsystem co-design for monolithic integrated devices.
  • Wide-/Ultrawide-bandgap Integrated Electronics — device, process, and RF integration research.
