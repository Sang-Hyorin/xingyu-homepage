Welcome

Building intelligent acoustic hardware for next-generation wireless systems.

Hi, everyone! 👋 Welcome to my homepage, and thank you for taking the time to learn more about me and my research.

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Electronic and Computer Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Yansong Yang.

My research brings together micro-acoustic devices, RF/mmWave circuits, and machine intelligence. Together with my groupmates, I develop resonators and filters that operate at higher frequencies, handle more power, and can be designed and measured more efficiently with data-driven methods.

My long-term goal is to bring intelligent manufacturing into every stage of micro- and nanofabrication, enabling smarter and more modern mass production of MEMS devices for communications, 6G, aerospace, and other emerging applications.
